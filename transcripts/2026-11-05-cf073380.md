**The theming tension, named.** Section 2.7 is about reference data coming in clusters over a surface where class is spatially autocorrelated. Two things in remote sensing make neighbours alike: the land cover itself comes in fields and forests, and the signal is autocorrelated too, through illumination, haze and terrain. The pizza already gives you the first one for free. Blotches model the second, but the page presents them as the cause, and "patches of tint like uneven lighting" is a photography metaphor pasted onto a kitchen metaphor. That is the weirdness you felt.

**On your two alternatives.** "Certain slices have certain toppings" is the right *explanation* but a poor *dial*. I tried 05's clumped-toppings setting here. The leak is biggest when toppings are messy at the scale between neighbour and square, which is what the paper pizza already is. "Baked differently" is the better home for the existing noise: an oven's hot side browns one region, chars another, and that is exactly the illumination analogue the blotch code implements.
