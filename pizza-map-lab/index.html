Pizza Map Lab

Interactive explainers for Stehman & Foody's Key issues in rigorous accuracy assessment of land cover products (2019). A classifier maps the toppings on a pizza, a taster checks a sample, and we work out how good the map really is. Every explainer starts from this same sheet pizza, cut into 64 squares like a raster cut into image chips, and baked so its map's mistakes match the paper's own worked example (Table 5) exactly.
